Company Return Policy:
Customers can return any product within 14 days of purchase, provided the item is unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Shipping Policy:
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within Pakistan. Express shipping is available for an additional fee and takes 1-2 business days. We currently ship to all major cities including Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, and Abbottabad.

Warranty Policy:
All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.

Customer Support:
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Customers can reach us via email at support@company.com or through the live chat on our website.
